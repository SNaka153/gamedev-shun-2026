How the canvas works

fillRect() draws boxes, ellipse() draws ovals, lineTo() joins points, and fillText() adds lettering. The star repeats ten points, alternating between a large and small radius.

Mirror flips the x-coordinate across the center using width - x. Four-way also flips y using height - y. Undo stores a pixel snapshot before each change.
