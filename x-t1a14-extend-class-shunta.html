BEHIND THE ISLAND
Related. Never identical.
Three child classes extend one parent: myAnimal. They share some behavior and change other behavior.
extends
class myCat extends myAnimal makes the cat a child of the animal class. It can use the parent's methods.
Inherited methods
Rest and Birthday are defined once in myAnimal. All three animals use those same methods.
Overriding
Each child defines its own myMove(). The same method call follows a ground, sky, or underwater route.
super
The bird and fish call super(name, age) in their constructors before adding their own properties. The cat needs no custom constructor. All three use super.myShow() to include the parent's introduction.
A new child method
Only myBird adds mySpeak(). Select Pip to try Chirp.
