01 / Meet the species

Five whales. Five stories.

Select a name to explore its reference length.

  1. Humpback whale

    Long flippers and individual tail markings help make each humpback distinctive.

  2. Killer whale

    Also called an orca. It belongs to the dolphin family.

  3. Blue whale

    The largest animal known to have lived on Earth feeds mainly on tiny krill.

  4. Gray whale

    A North Pacific traveller with a mottled body and long seasonal migrations.

  5. Sperm whale

    The largest toothed whale searches deep water for food, including squid.
